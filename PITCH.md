## 1. Upwork / Contra listing

**Title:** Menu price and delivery-app check for your restaurant: where you're losing customers on value

**Overview**

Customers now compare the total they pay, not the menu price. A $12 plate turns into $20 once a delivery app adds its markup, fees and tip, and a family of four picks whichever spot feeds them for less.

I check your menu the way a hungry, budget-minded customer would, using the all-in price per person, and show you exactly where you win, where you lose, and what to change.

**What you get**

- **Value per person vs. 5 nearby restaurants.** What it costs to feed 1 person and a family of 4 at your place, compared with the five closest competitors in your category, all-in (menu, fees, tax, tip).
- **App vs. pickup check.** How much more customers pay for your food on DoorDash or Uber Eats than through your own ordering page, and whether your pickup price is the obvious best deal.
- **Promo check.** Every special or BOGO you run, graded *Real deal*, *Small saving* or *Not worth it*, with the reason. Some app promotions cost customers more than ordering pickup at your regular price.
- **Portions and family options.** Whether your menu says how many people a dish feeds, and whether you're missing a family pack your competitors have.
- **3 specific changes**, ranked by how much they'd improve your value per person, each with the numbers behind it.

Delivered as a short report (about 2 pages) plus a 20-minute call to walk through it.

**How it works**

1. You send your current menu or ordering link.
2. I collect prices from your menu and nearby restaurants' own menus, and check app pricing by hand. No scraping.
3. You get the report within [X] business days.

**What I won't do**

- I won't promise rankings. My comparison site, Menu Math, ranks restaurants only by value per person, the same way for everyone. Buying a report doesn't change where you rank; acting on it can.
- I won't make up numbers. Anything estimated is labeled as an estimate.

## 4. Questions restaurants will ask

**"If I pay, will you rank me higher?"**
No. Rankings come only from value per person, the same way for every restaurant, and the method is published on the site. A report helps you see what to change; the changes are what move you up. If I sold rankings, the site would be worthless to customers, and so would being ranked well on it.

**"What if my food is better but costs more?"**
That's real, and the report says so. Value per person is one thing customers compare, not the only one. Ratings sit right next to the price on every card, and the report can include fixes that don't mean cutting your prices: a family pack, a lunch size, clearer portions, a pickup-only special.

**"Where do your numbers come from?"**
Your own menu and ordering page, and nearby restaurants' own menus, checked by hand with the date. App prices are estimated and labeled that way unless I've checked an actual checkout.

**"Can I get my specials featured?"**
Later, yes, in a clearly labeled Featured spot, and they still get the same deal check as everyone else.
