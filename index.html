How the numbers work

  • All-in price = menu prices + delivery fee + service fee + small-order fee + tax + tip. Pickup has no fees or tip.
  • Portions come from what the restaurant says first, then customer reports, then a typical amount for that kind of dish. Every card says which one it used.
  • Prices are estimates. Delivery fees change with distance, time and promotions, so your checkout may differ by a dollar or two.
  • Best value compares rating to cost per person. A 3-star place adds no value; each star above that does.
