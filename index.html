How the numbers work

  • All-in price = menu prices + delivery fee + service fee + small-order fee + tax + tip. Pickup has no fees or tip.
  • Restaurant prices are real, copied by hand from each restaurant's own website or ordering page on Nov 22–23, 2026. Restaurant-specific charges, like a card surcharge or a to-go container fee, are included when the menu states them. Check the restaurant before you go.
  • App prices are estimates. DoorDash and Uber Eats prices aren't collected. Restaurants often price items higher on the apps; an industry study put the average near 20%. TrueTab adds 20% to the restaurant's own prices, then typical fees. Real fees change with distance, time and promotions.
  • Portions come from what the restaurant says first, then customer reports, then a typical amount for that kind of dish. Every card says which one it used.
  • Kids count as 0.6 of an adult portion. Kids' meals are used one per kid when that's cheaper, and never feed adults.
  • Deals count only on the days, hours and ways to order they apply to. TrueTab works out the order with and without the deal and keeps the cheaper one.
  • Buy one, get one deals are marked with a BOGO badge. Restaurant BOGOs come from their menus; DoorDash and Uber Eats BOGOs are entered by hand when seen in the app, with an end date, and disappear when they expire.
  • Deal check grades every deal: Real deal (saves 10% or more with no warning signs), Small saving, or Not worth it. It compares the deal with the same order without it, with the cheapest way to order without any deal, with similar dishes nearby, and with the restaurant's recent prices.
  • Fair for every restaurant. Rankings and deal verdicts come only from this math, the same way for every restaurant. Nobody can pay to change a ranking, a price or a verdict. Anything paid will be labeled.
  • Reviews link to each restaurant's Google, Yelp and Tripadvisor pages side by side. Live star ratings are planned.
  • Best value compares rating to cost per person. A 3-star place adds no value; each star above that does.
